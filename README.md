# Sales Dashboard

## Final Report

The **Sales Dashboard** provides an overall view of sales performance, helping users monitor revenue, profitability, sales volume, regional performance, product performance, and salesperson contribution.

### Dashboard Screenshot

![Sales Dashboard Final Report](sales_dashboard_screenshot.png)

## Overview

The dashboard brings together key sales information into a single report so that business performance can be reviewed quickly and effectively.

It helps users understand:

* Overall sales and profitability
* Sales performance by region
* Product performance
* Salesperson contribution
* Units sold and sales volume
* Sales trends and performance patterns

## Key Metrics

The dashboard focuses on important KPIs such as:

* **Total Sales** – Overall revenue generated from sales.
* **Total Profit** – Profit generated after accounting for the cost of goods.
* **Units Sold** – Total quantity of products sold.
* **Number of Transactions** – Total sales transactions recorded.
* **Average Sales** – Average revenue generated per transaction.

## Business Analysis

The dashboard can be used to answer questions such as:

* Which region generates the highest sales?
* Which products perform best?
* Which salesperson contributes the most revenue?
* Which products have the highest sales volume?
* How profitable are the sales?
* How does sales performance change over time?

## Calculation Logic

```text
Total Sales = Units Sold × Unit Price

Total Cost = Units Sold × Cost of Goods

Profit = Total Sales − Total Cost

Average Sales = Total Sales ÷ Number of Transactions
```

## Data Used

The dashboard is based on transaction-level sales information including:

* Date
* Salesperson
* Region
* Product
* Units Sold
* Unit Price
* Cost of Goods
* Total Sales
* Profit

## Purpose

The main objective of the Sales Dashboard is to provide a clear and consolidated view of business performance. It enables stakeholders to quickly identify strong-performing products, regions, and salespeople while supporting data-driven decisions and performance monitoring.

## Conclusion

The Sales Dashboard converts detailed sales information into an easy-to-understand visual report. By combining key KPIs with sales analysis, it provides a practical overview of revenue, profitability, volume, and performance.
